# Social foundation

Stage D adds people, follows and a workout feed. This page says what exists, how privacy is enforced, and what is not built yet.

## What people can do

- **Profile (opt-in).** Nobody appears anywhere until they pick a handle at `/social/profile`. A profile has:
  - a handle: 3 to 20 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores, unique, with some names reserved;
  - an optional bio of up to 160 characters;
  - an optional picture;
  - a visibility, private by default;
  - a "let people find me" switch.
- **Follow.** A public account can be followed directly. A private account gets a follow request, which it accepts or declines at `/social/requests`. When an account switches to public, its pending requests are accepted.
- **Find people.**
  - Search by name or handle at `/social/search`.
  - The feed suggests people that your follows follow, built only from follower lists you are allowed to see.
- **Share a workout.** The Share button on a finished workout (`/history/[id]`) posts it with a caption and an audience: everyone, followers, or only me. Sharing the same workout again updates the post instead of making a second one.
- **Feed, likes and comments.**
  - `/social` shows your posts and the posts of people you follow, newest first, 20 at a time.
  - Comments allow one level of replies.
  - A post's author can remove any comment on it. Everyone else can remove only their own.
- **Block.** Blocking hides both people from each other everywhere: profile, search, feed, posts and comments. It ends follows and requests in both directions and removes their likes. The blocked person isn't told.
- **Report.** You can report a person, post or comment. The report keeps a copy of what was reported, so removing it afterwards doesn't remove the evidence. Only the reporter can read their own reports. Reviewing reports belongs to the moderation work in Stage E.
- **Leave social.** This deletes the profile, posts, comments, likes, follows and picture. Workouts are untouched.

## What a post shows, and what it never shows

The server builds the post from the saved workout (`src/modules/social/summary.ts`), so a device can't post numbers it didn't log.

- **Shown:** workout name, duration, exercises with set counts and best set, working sets, volume, and records.
- **Never shown:** notes, RPE, body weight, measurements, photos and nutrition.

When a workout is corrected, its post updates in the same transaction. When a workout is deleted, its post goes with it.

## How privacy is enforced

Privacy is enforced in the database, not only in the app.

- **Row-level security on every social table.** Reads and writes run as the signed-in person through `withUser`, so a bug in app code can't reveal a post the rules hide.
- **SECURITY DEFINER rule functions:**
  - `social_blocked`, `social_follows`;
  - `social_can_see`: a public account, a follower, or yourself, and never across a block;
  - `social_can_see_post`, which adds the post's own audience.

  These functions only answer yes or no, so the policies can check relationships without exposing the tables they read.

- **`profile_cards` view.** Other people's profiles are read only through this view (security barrier, owner rights). It carries the handle, display name, bio, picture and visibility, and nothing else from the private `profiles` table: no birth date, height, goals or units.
- **Database triggers:**
  - a block removes follows, requests and likes in both directions;
  - a reply must belong to the same post and can only reply to a top-level comment.
- **Avatars.** Pictures live in a private `avatars` bucket, one folder per person, and are shown through short-lived signed URLs.
- **Rate limits** (`src/modules/ops/rate-limit.ts`):

  | Action               | Limit              |
  | -------------------- | ------------------ |
  | Follows and requests | 60 an hour         |
  | Posts                | 30 an hour         |
  | Likes                | 300 an hour        |
  | Comments             | 60 per 10 minutes  |
  | Reports              | 20 a day           |
  | Searches             | 120 per 10 minutes |

- **Data export and account deletion** cover all social data.

## Tests

- `src/modules/social/social.test.ts` (16 database tests):
  - visibility for public, private and only-me posts;
  - follow requests, blocks and reports;
  - post summaries built on the server;
  - post refresh after a correction;
  - suggestions limited to lists you may see.
- `e2e/social.spec.ts`: two people, one public and one private, go through profile setup, follow request, accept, share, like, comment and block. It also checks that the other person's body weight never appears.
- The export tests cover the new sections.

## Not verified here

The avatars bucket policies apply only on Supabase. They are not exercised by the local tests, which use a stand-in storage schema.
